Fitbit Air MCP

A personal, self-hosted tool that lets the owner's own Claude desktop app read the owner's own Google Health (Fitbit) data, read-only, on the owner's own Mac.

个人自用工具：让本人的 Claude 桌面应用在本机只读访问本人的 Google Health（Fitbit）数据。

It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.
